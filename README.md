# Chess Engine Tuner
Only relevant for alpha-beta or minimax engines like Stockfish, rather than purely-AI engines. This is a tuner for chess engines, at least I'm making it for mine. How strong a chess engine is revolves around its evaluation ability. Good engines can predict a win, a draw or a loss in a balanced game from over 20 moves before the factual game ending. the way they do it is by looking at piece placement, counting material and assuming the opponent will make the best move that the engine can think of (there are other benchmarks, like king safety, pawn structure and piece activity, but it's more advanced). One of the ways Ferrous improved throughout the last few sub-version was me adjusting those metrics and manually testing playing precision and thinking time, which was of course inefficient and highly inaccurate, and although I managed to push it to beating me 80-90% of the games, it is still no match for contemporary programs. When I first learned about tuning, I thought "it's trivial, just make a script do what I did manually", but it turned out that 100 lines of code cannot make reasonable guesses on how to adjust the metrics, and I've had to immerse myself into the depths of tuning programs. I have no idea whether it has something to do with AI, nor do I understand some math formulae, because I skipped math at school, but the results are promising, and I am intrigued to explore a new field
